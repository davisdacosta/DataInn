Frequently asked questions

How long does delivery take?
Delivery isn't instant — bundles are sent to the network shortly after payment, and you'll see the order move to "delivered" on screen once it lands. Most orders complete within a few minutes.
Do I need an account to buy data?
No. Enter the recipient's number and your email at checkout — that's all that's required.
What happens if my payment succeeds but delivery fails?
If we can't deliver the bundle, your order is refunded automatically and you'll see the updated status on your order page.
Which payment methods can I use?
Payment is handled by Paystack, which supports Ghanaian debit/credit cards and mobile money.
Can I buy data for someone else's number?
Yes — the recipient number doesn't need to match your own. Just make sure it's entered correctly, since bundles can't be redirected once sent.
